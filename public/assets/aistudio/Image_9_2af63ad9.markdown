## Brand & Style
This design system marries the timeless tactile heritage of Indian handlooms with sophisticated generative AI tooling. It caters to discerning textile collectors, wedding curators, fashion enthusiasts, and developers building conversational and visual search experiences during Hacktoberfest. 

The aesthetic treats the interface as an illuminated glass drape: translucent woven-ivory panels, diffused peacock-emerald ambient reservoirs, and gilded 1-pixel antique gold hairlines that evoke pure Zari threadwork. The emotional tone is reverent yet highly analytical—delivering seamless AI visual recognition and conversational tailoring without compromising on the luxury, sensory richness, and cultural gravitas of traditional sarees (such as Kanjeevarams, Banarasis, and Chanderis).

## Colors
The palette balances vibrant heritage jewel tones with warm, breathable organic surfaces:
- **Primary (`#006D68` - Peacock Green):** Anchors primary brand actions, focused AI states, high-priority icons, and critical visual pathways.
- **Secondary (`#D9B879` - Antique Gold / Zari):** Used for micro-borders, gilded hairline accents, active toggles, badges of authenticity (e.g., Silk Mark certified), and search highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#102F30` - Deep Forest Green):** The supreme high-contrast tone for primary text labels, dark contextual modals, and deep grounding headers.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F5F0E7` - Warm Ivory) & Base Canvas (`#FFFCF6` - Soft Cream):** Replaces harsh stark whites with authentic raw tussar silk warmth.
- **Accent Muted Sage (`#DCE8DE`):** Employs subtle tinting for secondary button backgrounds, input fills, and AI tag pills.

Translucent alphas are strictly defined:
- Gold Hairline: `rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.35)`
- Ivory Glass Layer: `rgba(245, 240, 231, 0.72)`
- Emerald Glass Layer: `rgba(0, 109, 104, 0.08)`

## Typography
Typography reflects an editorial balance: the grandeur of bespoke atelier publications combined with ergonomic, legible UI data streams.
- **Headlines:** Set exclusively in `Playfair Display`. Weights 500 and 600 deliver authentic craft authority without losing delicate serifs on high-density retina displays.
- **Body & Controls:** Rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its open counter forms, clean geometries, and modern kerning balance the expressive display typography, guaranteeing effortless scanning of drape classifications, weave density metrics, and conversational prompt threads.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with slight positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mimic architectural luxury inscriptions on AI confidence indicators and weave tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, transitioning to a 6-column grid on tablets, and a 4-column structured stack on mobile. Outer margins are generous to instill an airy, boutique luxury feel reminiscent of high-end fashion catalog spreads.

Horizontal spacing follows an 8-point baseline grid with selective 4-point micro-stops. Layout modules maintain ample breathing room around high-resolution textile imagery and generative AI prompt docks, ensuring the screen never feels crowded.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on a tactile blend of isomorphic glassmorphism, gold hairline boundaries, and warm organic drop shadows:
- **Surface Elevation 0 (Base):** Unbroken `#FFFCF6` canvas tinted with subtle ambient radial gradients of `#006D68` at 4% opacity in the upper viewport quadrants.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Shelf Glass):** Background `rgba(245, 240, 231, 0.65)`, backdrop-filter `blur(16px)`, enclosed with a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.35)`. Casts a diffused warm shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(16, 47, 48, 0.06), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating AI Dock & Flyouts):** Background `rgba(255, 252, 246, 0.82)`, backdrop-filter `blur(24px)`, framed with `1px` border in `rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.6)`. Shadow: `0 16px 40px -6px rgba(16, 47, 48, 0.12), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.18)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Modals & Visual Search Overlays):** Deep Forest Green glass `rgba(16, 47, 48, 0.92)`, backdrop-filter `blur(28px)`, with a `1px` perimeter border in `rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.5)`. Text inverts to `#FFFCF6` and `#D9B879`.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of 2 (`0.5rem` / 8px default radius) provides an architectural, tailored softness without lapsing into toy-like or purely spherical geometries. Larger layout units (hero cards, glass overlays, and visual search modals) scale to `rounded-lg` (16px) and `rounded-xl` (24px) to retain structural poise against the curved contours of saree drapes. Form controls, pills, and dynamic AI query chips utilize pill contours (`9999px`) to emphasize their interactive state.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Peacock Action):** Solid `#006D68` surface with `#FFFCF6` typography, subtle inset border `1px solid rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.4)`, and warm shadow. On hover, background deepens toward `#102F30` with elevated gold edge luminescence.
- **Secondary (Gilded Outline):** Glass translucent fill `rgba(245, 240, 231, 0.5)`, `1px solid #D9B879`, text `#006D68`. On hover, fills with `rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.15)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or fill. `#006D68` text with subtle gold underline animation on hover.

### AI Search & Conversational Dock
- Floating translucent bar centered at the screen base or top discovery header. Glassmorphic pill (`rgba(255, 252, 246, 0.85)` + 20px blur) bordered by 1px `rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.45)`.
- Features visual upload triggers (camera / saree swatch drag-and-drop), conversational input field, and generative prompt suggestions with pulsating gold micro-badges.

### Saree Discovery Cards
- Glass background (`rgba(245, 240, 231, 0.7)`), 1px hairline border (`#D9B879` at 30%), soft inner padding.
- Card includes an edge-to-edge curved imagery viewport, region tag (e.g., "Varanasi", "Kanchipuram"), AI match percentage badge in muted sage, and subtle hover zoom with an intensified Zari border glow.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Base: Height 32px, pill-shaped, background `rgba(220, 232, 222, 0.5)`, text `#102F30`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 109, 104, 0.15)`.
- Active: Background `#006D68`, text `#FFFCF6`, border `1px solid #D9B879`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height 44px, background `rgba(255, 252, 246, 0.6)`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.4)`. Text `#102F30`, placeholder `#102F30` at 45% opacity. Focus state engages `1.5px solid #006D68` with an ambient peacock green outer ring blur (3px at 20% opacity).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkbox: Rounded 4px, `1.5px solid #D9B879`. Active fill `#006D68` displaying an antique gold check mark.
- Radio: Concentric dual-ring design. Checked state exhibits `#006D68` center dot with an antique gold outer ring border.

### Visual Attribute Inspectors (AI Textile Tags)
- Specialized metadata drawer detailing weave structure, warp/weft counts, and motif symbolism (e.g., Paisley, Peacock, Temple border). Rendered in a frosted glass panel featuring hairline divider rules in `rgba(217, 184, 121, 0.25)`.
